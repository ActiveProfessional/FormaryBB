# Marketing & Go-to-Market — Lakeside Roasters

Localized to the region and community; personalized to the founder's actual strengths and
relationships. If a section is irrelevant to the business type, stub with a pointer (see playbooks).

## Launch sequence »

The ordered first 90 days: pre-launch (list build, teaser, partners), launch (announcement,
first customer push), post-launch (reviews, referrals, repeat). Each step a P5 task.

## Channels »

## First customers

The first 10 (or 100, per type) by name, type, or source — concretely. Warm contacts list
(founder relationships from P0 profile) and how they get activated. Nobody's first customer
base should be "social media".

## Content & outreach »

What gets published, where, at what cadence — sized to actual capacity. What the AI stack
(opensearch personas in openwebui, langflow scheduling) will draft vs. what the founder
signs personally.

## Sales motion »

How a lead becomes a customer: inquiry → response time → quote/booking → close → onboarding.
Scripts/templates live in `source/` or the tool stack; this section defines the motion.

## Referrals & community »

The ask, the incentive (if any), the thank-you. Local reputation mechanics for local types
(reviews strategy, response protocol).

## Metrics & follow-up rhythm »

Which marketing numbers get tracked in `metrics.md` (leads, conversion, CAC by channel),
reviewed weekly. Channels that miss twice get cut — state the rule now, while it's cheap.
