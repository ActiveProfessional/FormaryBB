# Business Plan — Lakeside Roasters

**Reading order:** this master plan first; supporting docs in this folder on demand.
Reference conventions: facts `F-nnn`, assumptions `A-nnn`, risks `R-nnn`, decisions `D-nnn`,
tasks `T-nnn` — all live in registers, never duplicated here.

Fill every section unless your playbook marks it **~** (stub it with a one-paragraph summary
and a pointer), and deepen every **»** section. Length is not the goal; a stranger executing
the business from this document is.

## 1. Executive summary

What we sell, to whom, where, why now, how money moves, year-one objective. Written **last**.

## 2. Mission, goals, and objectives

From `00-goals-mission.md` — link, don't repeat. Objectives: measurable, dated, owned.

## 3. Concept & offering

Problem → solution → offering. What exactly the customer buys. Pricing and packaging.

## 4. Market & customers »(local/niche types go deep)

Summarize `10-market-research.md` (link). Target segments with evidence IDs. Region story.

## 5. Competition »(most types)

From `30-competition-analysis.md`: competitors, positioning, honest edge, pricing context.

## 6. Operations model »(op-heavy types go deep)

How the business runs day to day: production/service delivery, suppliers, hours, location,
tools, quality. Who does what, weekly.

## 7. Marketing & go-to-market »

From `40-marketing-gtm.md`: launch sequence, channels, first customers by name or type,
referral and community strategy, sales motion, follow-up rhythm.

## 8. Brand »

From `50-brand-identity.md`: name, voice, visual identity, tagline.

## 9. Team & roles

Who does what now (including "AI-operable stack" roles from P4); hires and when; advisors.

## 10. Technology & systems

From `../plan/40-tech-infrastructure.md` — link. What runs the business, and who maintains it.

## 11. Financials »

From `20-financial-plan.md` — link + summary table here: 12-month cash, break-even, unit
economics, run rate. No orphan numbers: every figure traces to `F-nnn`/`A-nnn`.

## 12. Risks & mitigation

From `registers/risks.md` — link + the top 5 by expected impact, each with mitigation status.

## 13. Local & personal details »(localized types go deep)

The stuff that makes this business *this* business: geography, community ties, founder
story, named relationships, personal constraints honored by the plan.

## 14. Plan follow & amendment (required)

How the plan is followed: operating cadence (weekly review, monthly snapshot, quarterly
gate), metric targets with watch-levels, who reviews what when, and the amendment rule
(quarterly variance ≥10% → amend via supersede convention). Include the first review dates.
