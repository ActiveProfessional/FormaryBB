# Jurisdictions — starting map (verify before relying)

**Purpose:** seed the compliance register with *candidate* obligations. This map is a
starting point for research and question-drafting — **it is never the last word**. Every
jurisdiction row that matters to a business gets a verification task (primary government
source or licensed professional) before it drives action or spend.

## How to use

1. At P1/P2, scan the sections matching the business's region(s) → seed `registers/compliance.md`
   with C-nnn rows (status TODO, source = "playbook, unverified").
2. Convert every row into either a verification task (check the primary source) or a
   professional question (for the attorney/CPA engagement in P2).
3. Mark confidence in `business-facts.md` only after verification — never before.

## United States (federal baseline)

| Category | Typical obligations |
|---|---|
| Entity | State-level formation (Secretary of State / equivalent), registered agent, annual report |
| Federal tax | EIN (IRS), federal income tax by entity type, self-employment tax |
| Employment | If hiring: payroll tax, workers' comp (state), unemployment insurance, labor law posters |
| Sales tax | State registration where nexus exists; marketplace rules for ecom |
| Data/privacy | FTC baseline; state laws (e.g., CCPA-type) if consumer data at scale; HIPAA only if health data |
| Industry | FDA/USDA for food production; SEC/FINRA for financial products; state licensing boards for trades |
| Product safety | CPSC; warning labels (e.g., CA Prop 65 for products sold into CA) |

## US state-flavor examples (directional, not exhaustive)

| State | Flavor notes |
|---|---|
| Wisconsin | LLC filing via DFI; city-level business registration (e.g., Milwaukee); straightforward annual report |
| California | Franchise tax on LLCs/Corps even at zero revenue; city business licenses; Prop 65 exposure |
| New York | LLC publication requirement (county-level) — a real cost line; NYC licenses layered |
| Texas | No state income tax; franchise tax thresholds; sales tax rules |
| Florida | Sunbiz filings; sales tax; local occupational licenses |
| Washington | B&O tax (gross receipts, not income); state L&I for employers |

## Other regions

| Region | Directional notes |
|---|---|
| Canada | Federal or provincial incorporation; CRA business number; GST/HST registration threshold |
| UK | Companies House for Ltd; HMRC self-assessment/VAT threshold; ICO registration for personal data |
| EU member states | Country-level formation; GDPR applies broadly; VAT/OSS registration for digital sales |
| Australia | ASIC; ABN; GST registration threshold; state fair trading rules |

## Professional verification (mandatory rows, any region)

- Entity choice + tax election → attorney and/or CPA
- Employment obligations at first hire → CPA/payroll service
- Industry licensing scope → licensing board or industry counsel
- Data/privacy applicability → counsel if consumer data is a real part of the model

## Multi-region / remote founders

- One region is **primary** (where the entity and the founder sit); others become tasks.
- Selling across borders (digital products especially) creates *nexus* questions — flag
  early, verify with a professional, and register sales tax where required.
- Non-resident founders: visa/work authorization is a **blocking question at P0** if the
  founder intends to work in the country of operation — never assume.
