# Question seeds — interview & research questions that unlock the chain

Two uses: (1) **P0 intake interview** with the requestor; (2) **P1 research planning** —
turn each relevant open question into a research task with a budget and a stop rule.

## P0 intake seeds (ask the requestor)

**The idea:** What are you selling? Who is it for? Where will you sell it? Why this —
why you? What's the mission in your words (quote them)?

**The person:** What relevant skills, licenses, experience do you bring? What assets,
relationships, or reputation can this business use? What constraints — time available per
week, capital available, existing job, family, non-compete or employer IP issues? What
would success look like in 12 months? What would make you abandon this? What's your risk
appetite and money attitude (reinvest vs. income)?

**The place:** What region exactly? Anything regional that helps or hurts (regulations,
competition, community, seasonality)? Any niche quirks the business must respect?

**The money story:** Who pays, how much, how often? What does it cost you to deliver?
Any key figures already known (prices, costs, market sizes)? Any price anchors from
comparables?

**The rules:** Any licenses, permits, or professional qualifications you know you need?
Any you suspect? Anything regulated about this idea (money of others, health claims, minors)?

**The stack:** Any tech preferences or constraints? (Default = internal openwebui +
openterminal + langflow unless the user says otherwise.)

## P1 research question patterns (per research area)

| Area | Question patterns | Evidence bar |
|---|---|---|
| Market size | How many buyers exist in scope? What do they spend today? | Secondary + triangulated est. |
| Demand | Do target customers recognize the pain? What do they do about it now? | Primary contact ≥ playbook minimum |
| Price | What would they pay? What do substitutes cost? | Quote + behavior, not surveys of intent |
| Competition | Who serves them now? Where are the gaps? | Fact rows; the do-nothing row included |
| Channel | Where do they gather, buy, ask for recommendations? | Verified places, not guesses |
| Regulation | What's required to operate legally? | Primary source or professional |
| Economics | What does a unit cost to deliver? Break-even at what volume? | Math shown, est. labeled |

## Question hygiene

- Every question in `registers/questions.md` needs a `Block` flag (which gate it blocks).
- A question answered by the user = fact row (source: founder, date).
- A question answered by research = fact row (source cited) + question row closed with the ref.
- Unanswerable-but-important → assumption row with kill-test (that's how it becomes answerable).
- **Research budget rule:** each P1 research task carries an hour budget; when the budget is
  spent without changing the verdict, stop and record what's known.
