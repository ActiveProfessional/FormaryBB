# Market Research — <Name>

Every number is a row in `registers/business-facts.md` first, referenced here as `F-nnn`.
Unverified numbers are `est.` and tied to an `A-nnn`. No naked numbers.

## Market definition

What market are we actually in (the smallest honest definition), geographic scope, and
how a stranger would size it.

## Trends & timing

3–5 trends that matter to *this* business, each sourced. Why now — or why not yet.

## Target segments »

2–4 segments. For each: description, need, where they gather (channels/places), willingness
to pay, and **evidence** — the F-nnn rows behind the belief. Evidence quality per the
business-type playbook minimum (primary contact > observed behavior > proxy data > theory).

## Customer evidence log

Summary of conversations/observations (no PII in this repo — names and contact details stay
with the founder; `source/` for shareable raw notes). What was asked, what was learned, F-nnn.

## Regional / niche factors

What about this *place* (or this *niche*) changes the answer: local competition density,
regulatory quirks, community dynamics, seasonality.

## The do-nothing alternative

How the target customer solves this today, and why that's tolerable or intolerable to them.
The real competitor is usually the status quo.

## Implications for the business

What this research changes about the P0 picture: pricing, segment choice, region, offering.
Changes recorded as `D-nnn` decisions.

**Break-even-or-bust (mandatory, feeds G1):** break-even volume ÷ segment size ÷ realistic
capture rate = plausibility. State it here; the verdict rides on it.
