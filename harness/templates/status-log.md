# Status Log — <Name>

Monthly one-pagers by the orchestrator: what changed, what's coming, what broke, what we
learned. The long-memory complement to the registers (which hold data, not narrative).

## <YYYY-MM> — <month theme>

- **Moved:** tasks/features/milestones completed (IDs)
- **Learned:** insights, surprises, killed assumptions (IDs)
- **Broke:** incidents, misses, and what changed because of them
- **Next:** the 30-day focus
- **Money:** cash position vs. plan (est. OK), upcoming obligations

(Repeat the section monthly. Old months stay — this is the business's memory, not a scratch pad.)
