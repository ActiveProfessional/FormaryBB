# P7 — Operate

**Purpose:** run the business per the plan, measure against targets, keep the repo truthful.
Operating is a loop, not a line — the repo is the business's memory and its control panel.

**Starts when:** launch-day actions complete (after G5). **Exits:** never — or at graduation
(sold, merged, closed with record). **Rhythm:** weekly / monthly / quarterly.

## Operating loop

```
WORK (tasks) → MEASURE (metrics) → REVIEW (rituals) → ADJUST (tasks, proposals, amendments) → repeat
```

## Steps

1. **Metrics live** → `metrics.md` (template): actuals vs. targets, weekly or monthly per
   the business-type playbook. Every metric has a source (statement, platform dashboard,
   hand count) — no vanity metrics without a decision they inform.
   - **Minimum metric set (every operating business, every type):** cash position (the
     monthly standing row — runway months), revenue or its mission-equivalent (beneficiaries
     served, events held, grants won), and customer pipeline or its mission-equivalent
     (leads, inquiries, applications). Type-specific metrics layer on per playbook; the
     minimum three are the floor that keeps the quarterly gate honest.
2. **Task rhythm** — work the register top-down; new work enters as tasks with IDs, never as
   chat promises. Blocked > due-date risk → surface in the weekly review, not in silence.
3. **Rituals** (see RUNBOOK.md): weekly review (user), monthly snapshot + `validate`,
   quarterly gate (variance ≥10% → plan amendment; compliance expiry sweep; decision
   consistency check; calendar archive/confirm).
4. **Records hygiene** — receipts/invoices to `source/`, bookkeeping entries per the accounting
   decision, contract copies to `source/`, renewal dates as C-nnn rows with calendar events.
5. **Incident & learning log** → `status.md` monthly entries: what happened, what changed.
   Wins and failures both — both are data for the quarterly review.
6. **Amendments** — plans change via the supersede convention, driven by variance triggers
   and P6 proposals. Versioned, dated, explained. Never quietly edited.

## Operating principles

- **The register is the truth.** If it's not in the repo, it didn't happen; if the repo says
  it and reality disagrees, fix the repo — with a decision logged.
- **Deadlines breathe only via decisions.** Slipping a date without a logged reason is drift;
  drift compounds. Re-baseline openly.
- **Money first.** Cash runway and receivables get checked before any other metric —
  small businesses die of cash, not of strategy.
- **Small steady beats big occasional.** Weekly honest hours beat heroic quarterly weekends.

## Done when

The loop runs for a quarter without register/reality drift; metrics inform decisions at
least once per quarter; compliance items never lapse (calendar + register double-check).

## Failure modes

- Metrics theater — tracking numbers nothing depends on.
- Repo decay — the plan frozen at launch while the business moves on; the quarterly gate
  exists to force the catch-up.
- Deadline drift without decisions.
- Cash surprise — the one that kills.
- Operating without the user: [U] gates and one-way doors still apply in operations (spend,
  hires, contracts, pivots).

## Gate criteria

Quarterly gate per GATES.md: metrics vs. targets, compliance sweep, decision consistency,
plan amendments where variance demands them.
